## Active Decisions

- `docs/development-plan.md` is the roadmap; SESSION.md tracks state against it
- **ADR-0030 (Accepted 2026-10-20):** a record points outside the graph with `externalRefs` (an identifier in another system, no relation to it yet) and `attachments` (a verifiable coordinate: mandatory `contentHash` and `hashAlgorithm` as an IANA registry name; `revision` required for `Git` and `Lore`, constraint 18); attachments on fragments, `text` optional, a fragment has text or an attachment (constraint 19); the graph never holds bytes and the hub does not verify them yet; both are `infrastructure` subdocuments whose nested ids are never stored; a change to a fragment's attachment is a change to the part (staleness sees it); the graph projects an asset store's history, never replays it (ADR-0025 amended)
- **ADR-0031 (Accepted 2026-10-20):** `GET /api/v1/export` is the graph at a ref as one JSON-LD document with its reloadable schema; profiles rewrite people only (`full`, `pseudonymous` with HMAC tokens under `ARMATURE_EXPORT_KEY`, `institutional`); slices are configuration, membership by class or ancestor, references leaving a slice reported as `unresolved` and never dropped; no import route, re-import is operator work through the adapter's operator-side methods; JSON-LD only; free text is not scrubbed
- **ADR-0063 (Accepted 2026-10-20):** a dataset or metric names its source, `producedBy`/`derivedFrom` or an `externalRef` (constraint 20); nothing is inferred about whose assessment it was; the seed carries one administration in a third commit with `asOf` the seed commit
- **ADR-0062 (Proposed 2026-10-20):** operator repair is a commit outside the plugin API; the store's enforcement is the floor; no superuser mode and no raw query route on `/api/v1`; three tools (`check`, `delete`, `put --unchecked`) reusing the adapter and the engine in-process; scratch branches deletable when named as such, design branches follow ADR-0025; `User` edits an operator task until a client needs a route. A dashboard is a client of `/api/v1`; only an admin tab over these tools sits with store credentials
- **The store's returned schema is not its accepted schema:** every `Set` comes back with `@max_cardinality: "inf"`, which the store refuses as `api:BadCast` (AE5); `exporter.normalizeSchema` drops it. Nested subdocument ids regenerate on re-import (AE7), so reproduction compares equal once `@id` is removed at every depth, as `fragments.canonical` does. A GET of a missing document is HTTP 200 with a 404 envelope in the body (AC4); the adapter reads the body
- **ADR-0056 (Accepted 2026-10-08): nothing derived is stored in the graph.** Coverage, alignment and any future score are computed by the intelligence reads at a ref; a judgment someone wants on record is a `DesignFinding` or an Attestation with a person's name. The schema holds what people decided and what was observed. Supersedes ADR-0007's computed field and ADR-0029 decisions 4 to 6
- **ADR-0029 decisions 1 to 3 (Accepted 2026-10-08):** coverage counts distinct items placed in the module's assessments, over two populations (ADR-0019); the default thresholds are the hub's provisional cut and the coverage read takes them as parameters and returns the counts. Formative uses (`ActivityItemLink`, ADR-0061) and unaligned items (ADR-0060) count toward nothing
- **ADR-0060 (Accepted 2026-10-20):** an item may exist before it is aligned: `assesses` is zero or more, rule 1 is retired, nothing at write time asks for alignment, and the alignment read lists `itemsWithoutObjective`. Alignment is encouraged, not required at the door; how the hub encourages it is a plan §6 question. Rule 2 stands until a client shows the activity case
- **ADR-0061 (Accepted 2026-10-20):** a question asked inside an activity is an `ActivityItemLink` (activity, item, optional `sequence`; Hash key over the pair; constraint 17), never an `ItemInstance`: no points, no clearance, no coverage. The trace follows it downstream; the module-content view nests it when built. Fields a client needs later are optional fields beside `sequence` (ADR-0010)
